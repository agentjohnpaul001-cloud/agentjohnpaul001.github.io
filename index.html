About the Program

DHHS Grants Program is a privately operated initiative focused on connecting people with information about assistance opportunities related to household needs, education, entrepreneurship, community development and support for vulnerable populations.

How to Get Started

Tell us what you need

Contact us with the general type of assistance you are looking for.

Review available opportunities

We can provide information about relevant opportunities and their published requirements.

Check the real provider

Before submitting sensitive information or paying any fee, verify the organization operating the specific program.

Apply through the appropriate channel

Follow the application instructions supplied by the actual program provider.

Want information about assistance opportunities?

Contact the program for general information. Please do not send passwords, bank PINs, card security codes, or other unnecessary sensitive information by email.
